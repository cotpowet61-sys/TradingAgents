Nature of the service

TradeVision is an analytics and education software service. It provides market data processing, indicator-based signal analytics, position-size mathematics and statistics on the user's own trading history. It is not a broker, it does not execute orders, it does not manage assets, and it does not provide investment advice or portfolio management within the meaning of the German KWG/WpHG or MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114) in the areas we operate in. Trading decisions are made solely and exclusively by the user.
