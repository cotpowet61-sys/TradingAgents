3. What we deliberately do NOT do

  • No trading, withdrawal or transfer permissions are ever accepted or stored — read-only keys are validated against the exchange and rejected if they carry trade/withdraw rights.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking pixels, no social plugins, no cross-site profiling, no data selling.
  • No automated decision-making producing legal effect (Art. 22 GDPR). Signal analytics is advisory output; you decide.
  • No custody of funds or keys: payments go directly to a public wallet address; private keys never exist on the server.

4. Recipients / processors

  • Hosting provider of the deployment (server, backups): [[HOSTING PROVIDER + LOCATION — ideally EU]]; DPA concluded.
  • Exchange public APIs (e.g. Bybit) — to fetch market data and, with your consent, your history. Your read-only keys are sent to the exchange for authentication, as technically required.
  • Blockchain explorers (Tronscan, BscScan) — public, watch-only queries of the receiving address to match your payment. Only public blockchain data and our own address are queried.
  • AI model provider (OpenAI-compatible endpoint configured by the operator, or a free community provider) — trade summaries and market snapshots may be sent to generate text analysis. Never your password, never full API keys, never withdrawal-relevant data. If you prefer no AI processing, disable the corresponding roles.
  • Telegram (if you use the bot) — Telegram is an independent controller for messages you send there.

Some processors (AI providers, Telegram, exchange APIs) may operate outside the EU/EEA. Where required, transfers rely on Art. 45 (adequacy) or Art. 46 (Standard Contractual Clauses) plus additional safeguards of the respective provider.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use only strictly necessary cookies/local storage: session, referral code, and UI preferences (sound/notifications, watchlist). No analytics or marketing cookies exist in this application, so no consent banner is required under § 25(2) TDDDG. If you add analytics later, this section must be updated and consent implemented.

8. Security

Transport encryption (HTTPS/HSTS via reverse proxy), password hashing with PBKDF2 (per-user salt, 260k iterations), session tokens stored only as hashes, AES-256-GCM encryption for user exchange keys, least-privilege process users, access-rate limits, audit logging of administrative and payment actions. No system is impenetrable — if you discover a vulnerability, please contact us first at [[SECURITY EMAIL]].
